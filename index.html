The person behind the byline

I'm Shrishti, 27 years old with an Eng Lit degree, which I'm still deciding was worth it.

A friend gave me a copy of Harry Potter when I was 12, and that was it, I was gone, off into the wizarding world, and I never really came all the way back.

Books and Journaling wasn't just a hobby, it was an escape from high school and bullying. I was in boarding school, so the only way to stay sane was writing down all my thoughts, about my life, but also about the world.

I could tell you I'm a very passionate writer, but the truth is that transitioning into a professional writer is still an ongoing process. Honestly, it feels like a costume I'm trying on. But here's the truth: it's the one thing I actually trust myself to do well. It's not merely a hobby anymore, it's an art form, one I'm planning to master someday.

When I'm not writing, you'll find me three coffees deep, watching YouTube commentary videos, or falling down a rabbit hole about some war or election, or honestly, anything that makes me curious.
